Mazey

Tutorial

Mazey is a game about solving perfect mazes, mazes with only one path to end, solve as many as you can by clicking on the orange boxes. Red denotes the end goal and green denotes squares already in your path, once you have reached the goal, the next maze will load. good luck solving!
